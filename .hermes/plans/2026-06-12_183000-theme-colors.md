# Modern Theme Colors

**Goal:** Modernize light and dark theme backgrounds, text, and panel treatments for a cleaner, more professional look.

---

| `--ft-panel-border` | `rgba(15,23,42,0.1)` | `rgba(15,23,42,0.06)` | Borde más suave |
